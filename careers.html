Where Do You Fit?

From your first day in the bay to running your own store, there's a path for you here.

Guest Service Advisor

The Guest Service Advisor educates our guests on the vehicle manufacturer's recommendations. Explains service benefits and offers services for their vehicle by utilizing the Otto Care system. Provides Superior Guest Service by exceeding their guest expectations while following and complying with Alamitos Enterprises Values Statement.

Lube Technician

The Lubrication Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube automotive preventive maintenance lubrication products and services, in a safe, accurate and timely manner while following and complying with Alamitos Enterprises Values Statement.

Advanced Technician

The Advanced Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube multicare approved services in a safe, accurate and timely manner while following and complying with Alamitos Enterprises, LLC Values Statement.

Master Technician

The Master Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube multicare approved services in a safe, accurate and timely manner while following and complying with Alamitos Enterprises, LLC Values Statement.

Assistant Manager

The Assistant Manager at Alamitos Enterprises, LLC is responsible for a wide range of critical duties that ensure the success of a Jiffy Lube location. This role includes managing store operations, personnel, guest services, safety, sales, and profitability, while also ensuring a positive work environment and high guest satisfaction.

Manager in Training

The Manager in Training for Alamitos Enterprises, LLC is responsible for a wide range of duties critical to the success of a Jiffy Lube location, including store operations, personnel management, facilities maintenance, guest service, safety, sales, and profitability, while adhering to the Alamitos Enterprises Values Statement. The Manager must oversee and develop staff, market the service center, ensure guest satisfaction, maintain cleanliness, control expenses, resolve guest complaints, and delegate tasks effectively to ensure overall success.

Manager

The Service Center Manager position at Alamitos Enterprises, LLC includes a wide variety of critical responsibilities for the success of a Jiffy Lube location, encompassing store operations, personnel management, facilities, guest service, safety, sales, and profitability, all while adhering to the Alamitos Enterprises Values Statement. Additionally, the Manager is responsible for overseeing staff, developing employees, marketing the service center, ensuring a positive work environment and guest satisfaction, and addressing guest complaints, while hiring and training competent management staff to achieve these objectives through proper delegation.
