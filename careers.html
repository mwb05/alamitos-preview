The path

Start in the bay, finish running the store

Every one of these is a rung, not a dead end. Most of our managers started at the bottom of this list.

01
Where you start, no experience needed

Lube Technician

The Lubrication Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube automotive preventive maintenance lubrication products and services, in a safe, accurate and timely manner.

02
Off the bay, in front of the guest

Guest Service Advisor

The Guest Service Advisor educates our guests on the vehicle manufacturer’s recommendations. Explains service benefits and offers services for their vehicle by utilizing the Otto Care system. Provides superior guest service by exceeding expectations.

03
More services, more responsibility

Advanced Technician

The Advanced Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube multicare approved services in a safe, accurate and timely manner while following and complying with the Alamitos Enterprises, LLC values statement.

04
The top of the technical track

Master Technician

The Master Technician performs service and installation activities associated with Jiffy Lube multicare approved services in a safe, accurate and timely manner while following and complying with the Alamitos Enterprises, LLC values statement.

05
First step into leadership

Assistant Manager

The Assistant Manager is responsible for a wide range of critical duties that ensure the success of a Jiffy Lube location. This role includes managing store operations, personnel, guest services, safety and sales.

06
Learning to run the whole store

Manager in Training

The Manager in Training is responsible for a wide range of duties critical to the success of a Jiffy Lube location, including store operations, personnel management, facilities maintenance, guest service and safety.

07
Your own store

Manager

The Service Center Manager position includes a wide variety of critical responsibilities for the success of a Jiffy Lube location, encompassing store operations, personnel management, facilities and guest service.
