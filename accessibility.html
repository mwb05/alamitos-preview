Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: November 12, 2026.

The Alamitos Group wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and the WCAG 2.2 additions where practical.

What we have done

  • Every page has a “Skip to content” link, a logical heading order, page landmarks and a visible keyboard focus indicator.
  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard alone, including the mobile menu and the reviews controls.
  • Images have text alternatives; price menus that appear as pictures also have a text version.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA colour-contrast ratios, and pages reflow without sideways scrolling down to 320 pixels wide and at 200 % zoom.
  • Animations are turned off when your device is set to reduce motion, and nothing on the site autoplays sound or video.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and required fields are announced by the browser.
  • Each page is tested with automated accessibility checks (axe-core) and by hand with the keyboard.

Known limitations

Some links lead to services run by others, such as Google Maps, ADP (job applications) and our employee tools. We cannot control their accessibility, but we will help you complete the same task another way: call (714) 236-0715 and we will help by phone.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in a different format, contact us and we will help you get it:

  • Email: cjohnson@officeamg.com
  • Phone: (714) 236-0715 (Monday–Sunday)

Please tell us the page and the problem you ran into. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to fix confirmed issues as quickly as we can.
