Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Alamitos Enterprises, LLC (“The Alamitos Group”) (“we,” “us”) collects, uses and shares personal information through this website, alamitosgroup.com (the “Site”), and the choices and rights you have, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CCPA”) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (“CalOPPA”). It covers this Site only. Information you give a store in person is handled under that store’s practices.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you use the “Send Us a Message” form or email us, we receive your first and last name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Phone calls. If you call a store or our office, we receive your phone number and what you tell us.
  • Technical information. Like every website, our hosting provider automatically receives your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the referring page and the date and time of the visit in its server logs.
  • Job applications. Our Careers page links to ADP, which receives and processes job applications under its own privacy policy. We receive the application information you submit there.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this Site, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information through it.

Categories of personal information (CCPA notice at collection)

In the last 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information through the Site:

  • Identifiers (name, email address, phone number, IP address). Source: you, and our hosting provider. Purpose: to answer your message, provide service and keep the Site secure. Disclosed to: our website host and form-delivery provider as service providers.
  • Internet or network activity (server log data described above). Source: your browser. Purpose: operating and securing the Site. Disclosed to: our website host as a service provider.
  • Professional or employment information (only if you apply for a job). Source: you, through ADP. Purpose: considering your application. Disclosed to: ADP as a service provider.

We keep messages only as long as needed to answer them and for ordinary business records, generally no longer than three years. Server logs are kept by our host for its standard period. Job application information is kept as employment law requires.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to you, schedule and provide service, consider job applications, operate and secure the Site, and comply with the law. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

How we share information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the Site and our business (such as our website host, GitHub; our form-delivery provider; and ADP for job applications), when the law requires it, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business. We do not sell personal information and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the last 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.

Third-party content. To display the Site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and an animation library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Those providers receive your IP address as part of delivering the file. Links to Google Maps, ADP and our employee tools take you to sites run under their own privacy policies.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know and access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes and the categories of recipients;
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it);
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect it through the Site); and
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, email cjohnson@officeamg.com or call (714) 236-0715. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days (we may extend once by another 45 days and will tell you why). To protect you, we verify your identity by matching information you give us with information we hold, such as the email address you wrote from. You may use an authorized agent; we will ask for your signed permission and may verify your identity directly. Under California’s “Shine the Light” law (Civil Code § 1798.83), we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.

Cookies and similar technologies (cookie notice)

This Site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or other trackers. Third-party providers that deliver fonts and scripts (Google Fonts, cdnjs) may log technical information as described above but are not used to track you. If we ever add cookies or analytics, we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. Because this Site does not track visitors over time or across other websites, it treats every visitor the same way whether or not the signal is sent. We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, which we do not do in any case. Third parties do not collect personal information about your online activities over time and across different websites through this Site.

Children

This Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The Site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and we limit who can read the messages we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive information through the contact form or email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: Alamitos Enterprises, LLC, email cjohnson@officeamg.com, phone (714) 236-0715.
