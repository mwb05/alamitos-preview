Our Services

Everything your vehicle needs to stay safe, efficient, and on the road longer.

Oil Change

Motor oil lubricates the mechanical parts that move inside your engine as well as helps to clean, cool and protect your engine. Jiffy Lube® recommends that you follow your vehicle manufacturer's recommendation for oil changes based on your individual driving habits and conditions.

Battery

Whether you need a battery tested, a new battery or the terminals cleaned, Jiffy Lube® offers the battery services you need to help keep your starting charging system running efficiently.

Brakes

From brake pads to brake fluid, Jiffy Lube® provides the services you need to help keep your stopping power responsive and reliable – and, get you back on the road quickly.

Engine

From check engine lights and spark plugs to serpentine belts and radiator hoses, Jiffy Lube® can help maintain your engine's optimal performance, reliability and longevity.

Rideshare Inspections

Jiffy Lube provides comprehensive rideshare vehicle inspections, ensuring your car meets all safety and maintenance standards required for rideshare services.

Filters

Whether filtering a fluid or the air, Jiffy Lube® filter services help ensure optimal performance and vehicle longevity.

Fluids

Jiffy Lube® fluid services help maintain your vehicle's performance in areas such as brakes, transmission, radiator coolant and more.

Inspections

For quick and convenient inspections and testing, Jiffy Lube® may offer the services you need to help ensure your vehicle meets local emissions and/or safety requirements.

Suspension

To help keep your ride smooth and stable, Jiffy Lube® offers a range of suspension services for your vehicle.

Tires

Whether your tires need rotating, balancing, repair or even replacement at some locations, Jiffy Lube® tire services will get you back on the road fast.
