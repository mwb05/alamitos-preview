The AlamitosStory

The Alamitos Group is one of Southern California’s largest and most established owner operators of Jiffy Lube service centers, but it hasn’t always been that way. Brothers-in-law Robert Curry and Michael Biddle started with humble beginnings in 1995.

Now a major player in Los Angeles County as a multi-million dollar industry and brand leader, the Alamitos Group is still proudly family owned and operated with deep-rooted community values.
