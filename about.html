The Alamitos Story

The Alamitos Group is one of Southern California's largest and most established owner operators of Jiffy Lube service centers but it hasn't always been that way. Brothers-in-law, Robert Curry and Michael Biddle, started with humble beginnings in 1995 with a single service center. They started their company with the notion that the communities in the Southland could benefit from convenient vehicle maintenance provided by well trained technicians and performed with a passion for great guest service.

Now a major player in Los Angeles County as a multi-million dollar industry and brand leader, the Alamitos Group is still proudly family owned and operated with deep-rooted community values.
