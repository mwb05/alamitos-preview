What guides us

How we run a service floor

Six values, one mission, and a standard we hold every store to — every guest, every time.

Mission

We are in business to

01

Build a long-lasting and financially sound enterprise.

02

Be an employer of choice, and give our team members opportunities to develop a meaningful career.

03

Help our guests keep their vehicles safe, efficient, and on the road longer.

Values

The six we hold to

These are the ones we hire against, promote against, and correct against.

01

Commitment to Team Members

Our team members are the heart of our business. We treat each other with the same respect and concern we expect them to show our guests.

02

Passion for Guests

Customers are guests, and their needs come first. As an empowered team we are devoted to giving them an exceptional service experience.

03

Uncompromising Integrity

We are open, honest and direct in all of our dealings — with guests, with vendors, and with each other.

04

Results

We plan for our goals and hold ourselves accountable to them. Every team member’s contribution is critical to hitting them.

05

Adaptive

We keep adjusting to the needs of our guests and our team, and stay current with the constant change in this industry.

06

Community

We are visible, active members of the communities we serve — not just a business with an address in them.

Vision

Where we’re headed

We give retail and fleet guests faster, more convenient preventative maintenance and repair of the highest quality — and we explain the manufacturer’s recommendation while we do it.

Owners are keeping their vehicles longer. Preventative maintenance and mechanical repair will keep growing, absorb more services, and draw more competition.

The Alamitos Group intends to be recognised as the premier operator in that space.

Work somewhere that lives its values
