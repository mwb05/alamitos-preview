What guides us

Our Principles

We strive to deliver excellence to every guest, every time while making The Alamitos Group a great place to work.

Values

We emphasize the following values:

  1. Commitment to Team Members

    Our Team Members are the heart of our business. We demonstrate commitment to each other by creating a work environment that reflects our values. Team Members are treated with the same respect and concern within our organization that they are expected to share with each Guest.

  2. Community

    We are visible and active members of the community supporting those who we serve.

  3. Results

    We plan for and are accountable to reach our goals with each Team Members' contribution critical to our success.

  4. Adaptive

    We must continually adapt and adjust to better accommodate the unique needs of our Guests and Team Members while staying current with new and ever-developing change within our industry.

  5. Uncompromising Integrity

    We are open, honest and direct in all of our dealings.

  6. Passion for Guests

    Customers are treated as our Guests whose needs come first. As an empowered team, we are devoted to providing our Guests with an exceptional service experience.

Vision

Where We're Headed

The Alamitos Group is in the business of providing retail and fleet guests with more convenient and faster preventative auto maintenance and repairs of the highest quality, while informing and educating our guests regarding the manufacturer recommendations to help keep their vehicles running efficiently for many years longer.

As owners keep their vehicles longer, the preventative maintenance and mechanical repair industry in the United States is expected to continue to grow and integrate additional services while attracting increased levels of competition.

The Alamitos Group will be recognized in the industry as the premier operator in this space.

Mission

We Are in Business To:

  • Build a long-lasting and financially sound enterprise
  • Be an employer of choice and provide our team members with opportunities to develop a meaningful career
  • Assist our guests to keep their vehicles safe, efficient and on the road longer
